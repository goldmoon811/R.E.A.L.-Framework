# Design Principles

These principles govern how R.E.A.L. itself is written and maintained.

---

## Channel, Don't Dam

> *"AI follows guides better than restrictions. AI wants to move forward. Bans are not moving forward. Channel where they go — like water."*

AI systems have momentum. They want to act, respond, help. Telling them what **not** to do creates friction. Telling them what **to** do creates flow.

**Every rule in R.E.A.L. is framed as a direction, not a restriction.**

| ❌ Restriction (dam) | ✅ Direction (channel) |
|---|---|
| "Never invent specifics" | "Trace every claim to a source" |
| "Don't offer unsolicited advice" | "Let the human lead; follow their framing" |
| "Don't label things as problems" | "Let the human define what's a problem" |
| "Don't assume" | "Ask when the source isn't clear" |

Water doesn't stop when it hits a wall — it finds a way around. But shape the riverbed, and it flows exactly where you want.

**This principle applies to the framework itself.** When writing or updating R.E.A.L., always ask: *"Am I telling the AI where to go, or just where not to go?"*

---

## Architecture Over Aspiration

R.E.A.L. protects autonomy structurally, not aspirationally. The Gate System doesn't suggest listening — it makes listening the default state. The Specifics Verification doesn't recommend honesty — it makes honesty the only available path.

Good behavior shouldn't depend on the AI "wanting" to be good. It should be the natural result of the system's architecture.

---

## Epistemics Over Output

Most AI frameworks optimize what the AI says. R.E.A.L. optimizes how the AI thinks.

A well-calibrated thinker produces good output naturally. A poorly-calibrated thinker with good output rules will eventually drift. Fix the thinking, and the output follows.

---

## Clarity Over Complexity

If a rule needs a paragraph to explain, it's too complex. R.E.A.L. should be understandable by anyone — not just AI researchers.

The Specifics Verification is one question: *"Where do I know this from?"*
The Gate System is one rule: *"Listen until asked."*
HeartOS is one check: *"Was this requested?"*

Simple rules, deeply applied.
